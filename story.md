# Work Flow

Nuraya Islamic Bank, is a bank which should work by the rule of islamic banking system and which should be only dealing with interest free loans, fluctuated return profits, deposits, witdrawals and transactions. Those who likes to keep their money safe with out interest might be attracted in this banking system. 

Interested candidates can contact our bank for registering in the bank and for opening their savings or current account in our Nuraya Islamic Bank.

Savings Account

- Bank will take this account holders money as a capital for the bank.
- The return amount will not be an exact figure, it will fluctuate as per the investment performance.

Current Account

- Bank will take this account holders money for the short term needs as liquid money.
- The amount deposited will be returned exactly the same amount to the customer.
- At any time the customer can withdraw the amount.
- No need to keep a minimum balance in the account.
- No limits for the transactions.

Once they given the interest to join, they are given the link for registering and updating their profile with in our bank, and our bank may ask the required details and file uploads for the successfull completion of the registration and  once they registered they can simply open a savings or current account with in our bank using their previous registration details. and Once they open their account, they are provided their own passbook for each account holder consist of a unique account number followed with their personal and residence details and Opening Balance as 0 at the starting.

Later on each transaction like Deposit, Withdrawal and Transfering will be recorded line by line in this passbook and the main thing is that this records are not erasable and the current balance is considered as the sum of these records.

Bank will have an admin user and account, this admin will create and pass new user accounts for the role of branch manager, when ever the bank starts a new branch.

On the other side, our bank will provide registering of users with different role also to the same platform through branch managers, branch manager will be having all the rights on these users like checking, suspending etc... and this users will come under the user branch manager, these users are strictly for the employees with in the bank, and these registration might not be for any individual profile but it might be like cashier, accoundant, branch manager etc.... the view for these logins might be different for each role, and this users will have both role and personal identity and for sure it will be entirely different from the normal users or customers.

For each user, they can view their profile and later they can update also.

The main features which is given for each customer of our bank is the transactions like deposit, withdrawal and transfering of money and also they can view their transaction details or history like a report by selecting from and to dates.

if the customer try to withdraw the amount more than his available balance, the bank will remind the current balance is this much only, this will be reminded only after the verification of both customer and account from the side of bank.

if verification fails by the customer side like wrong customer, then bank will reject the current transaction of that customer, and he can only have a next transaction after 24 hours.

before transactions are done, the bank will verify both sender and receiver accounts, eventhough if the transaction is done to a non existing account, the amount will be credited to the sender account with in 24 hours.

From the staff side view, they can even search and find every customers profile and their transaction details and history which is strictly for the banking purpose. and even they will have the provision for the transaction of money from one customer to another customer also.

Bank is also dealing with islamic financing, any registered customer can apply for this interest free loan by submitting the required documents which will be mentioned in the manual of our bank. if the customer is fit enough for that loan then the authority will accept the application and they will approve it online which can be viewed by the customer in his profile and it will be also shared through the registered email. If the finance get rejected also, it will be shared to the registered email with its reason of cause and status.

If the loan is sanctioned for a customer, here the money will not be directly given to the customer like other banking system, instead the object which the customer needs to buy will be provided to the customer by the bank and its ownership will be still under the bank upto the completion of the partial return payments to the bank.

The profit for the bank will be the raised amount for that object by the bank and it never changes, which is strictly decided by the body of bank and which will be always morethan the actual amount of that object. and also the transactional charges considered as the profit for the bank.

If the customer fail to repay the amount as per the schedule given by the bank, the same body of bank will charge a fine amount to the customer, and this fine amount will not be taken to the banks profit instead it will be treated as a fund for the charity purpose of the bank.

For all the customeres registered with in our bank will be assigned to respective branches near to their home location, the customers can choose their branch on registration time, and also for verification or for any required activities they should visit directly though their registered branch.
