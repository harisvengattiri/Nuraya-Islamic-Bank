# Work Flow

Nuraya Islamic Bank is a bank which should work by the rules of the Islamic banking system and should only deal with interest-free loans, fluctuating return profits, deposits, withdrawals, and transactions. Those who like to keep their money safe without interest might be attracted to this banking system.

Interested candidates can contact our bank for registering with the bank and for opening their Savings or Current Account in our Nuraya Islamic Bank.

Savings Account

* Bank will take this account holder's money as capital for the bank.
* The return amount will not be an exact figure; it will fluctuate as per the investment performance.

Current Account

* Bank will take this account holder's money for short-term needs as liquid money.
* The amount deposited will be returned exactly the same to the customer.
* At any time, the customer can withdraw the amount.
* No need to keep a minimum balance in the account.
* No limits on transactions.

Once they have given their interest to join, they are given the link for registering and updating their profile within our bank, and our bank may ask for the required details and file uploads for the successful completion of the registration. Once they are registered, they can simply open a Savings or Current Account within our bank using their previous registration details. Once they open their account, they are provided with their own passbook for each account holder, consisting of a unique account number followed by their personal and residential details, and an opening balance of 0 at the start.

Later on, each transaction like Deposit, Withdrawal, and Transfer will be recorded line by line in this passbook, and the main thing is that these records are not erasable, and the current balance is considered as the sum of these records.

Bank will have an admin user account. This admin will create and approve new user accounts for the role of Branch Manager whenever the bank starts a new branch.

On the other side, our bank will provide registration of users with different roles also on the same platform through Branch Managers. Branch Managers will have all the rights over these users, like checking, suspending, etc. These users will come under the respective Branch Manager. These users are strictly employees within the bank, and these registrations might not be for individual profiles but rather for positions like Cashier, Accountant, Branch Manager, etc. The view for these logins might be different for each role, and these users will have both role and personal identity. It will be entirely different from the normal users or customers.

For each user, they can view their profile and later they can update it also.

The main features given for each customer of our bank are transactions like Deposit, Withdrawal, and Transfer of money, and they can also view their transaction details or history as a report by selecting from and to dates.

If the customer tries to withdraw an amount more than the available balance, the bank will remind them that the current balance is only this much. This will be reminded only after the verification of both the customer and account from the bank's side.

If verification fails on the customer side, like a wrong customer, then the bank will reject the current transaction of that customer, and they can only have the next transaction after 24 hours.

Before transactions are done, the bank will verify both sender and receiver accounts. Even if the transaction is made to a non-existing account, the amount will be credited back to the sender's account within 24 hours.

From the staff side view, they can search and find every customer's profile and their transaction details and history, which is strictly for banking purposes. They will also have the provision for the transfer of money from one customer to another customer.

Bank is also dealing with Islamic financing. Any registered customer can apply for this interest-free loan by submitting the required documents mentioned in the bank's manual. If the customer is eligible for that loan, then the authority will accept the application and approve it online, which can be viewed by the customer in their profile and will also be shared through the registered email. If the finance gets rejected, it will also be shared through the registered email with the reason and status.

If the loan is sanctioned for a customer, the money will not be directly given to the customer like in other banking systems. Instead, the object which the customer needs to buy will be provided by the bank, and its ownership will remain with the bank until the completion of the partial repayment schedule.

The profit for the bank will be the increased amount for that object decided by the bank, and it never changes. This is strictly decided by the governing body of the bank and will always be more than the actual amount of that object. Also, the transactional charges are considered as profit for the bank.

If the customer fails to repay the amount as per the schedule given by the bank, the same governing body of the bank will charge a fine amount to the customer. This fine amount will not be taken as the bank's profit; instead, it will be treated as a fund for the charity purposes of the bank.

All customers registered within our bank will be assigned to their respective branches nearest to their home location. Customers can choose their branch at the time of registration, and for verification or any required activities, they should visit their registered branch directly.
